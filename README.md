# Rare Friends: Outbreak

Playable zombie-apocalypse demo for the Rare Friends vibeathon.

Repo: https://github.com/stevereynolds2006-ship-it/outbreak-demo

## Play

1. Download `outbreak-playable-demo.html` (self-contained).
2. Open it on your phone or computer.

- Supply locker — buy kits (1 RF)
- Weapons crate — pipe, crowbar, hatchet, shotgun
- Outbreak gate — fight 5 walkers, then roll the haul

Odds match the submission table. Max prize 10 RF. Demo RF is simulated.
